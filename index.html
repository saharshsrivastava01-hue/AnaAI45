Search & AdSense setup

This free static demo is not automatically eligible for AdSense. Google reviews the actual site, ownership, content and policy compliance. A GitHub Pages subdomain may not meet AdSense's normal site-signup requirements.

  1. Publish substantial original content and complete real About, Contact, Privacy and Terms pages.
  2. Use a domain and hosting setup accepted by AdSense; verify site ownership.
  3. Set up Google Search Console and submit the sitemap after public deployment.
  4. Apply at Google AdSense and wait for Google's review.

Google ranking, indexing time, approval and advertising revenue cannot be guaranteed.
