Privacy notice

Last updated: 26 November 2026

Current beta features

Saved drafts and the draft counter are stored in your browser's local storage on this device. The current prototype does not offer user accounts or cross-device synchronisation.

AI generation

When the AI backend is configured and you request a generation, the information you submit (such as topic, audience, platform, tone and proof point) is sent from your browser to the SparkLoop server and then to the OpenAI API to produce the response. The OpenAI API key is stored as a private server environment variable and is not sent to your browser. Do not submit passwords, payment details, confidential business secrets or sensitive personal information.

The SparkLoop API does not intentionally store the submitted prompt or generated text. If an app database is connected later, it may store limited service metrics such as platform, model and timestamp; any change to account or draft storage should be reflected in an updated notice.

Payments and sensitive data

This beta does not process payments or hold wallet funds. Never enter a UPI PIN, OTP, bank password or payment-card security code into this app.

Browser and hosting data

Clearing this site's browser storage removes locally saved drafts. Hosting and AI providers may process ordinary technical request information under their own applicable terms and privacy policies.

Contact

Add a monitored support email and legal business identity before inviting public users or accepting payments.
