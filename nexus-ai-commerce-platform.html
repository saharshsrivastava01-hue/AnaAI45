Platform settings & status

This build is a no-API-key local prototype. All app data is stored in this browser using localStorage.
  • Frontend: responsive HTML, CSS and JavaScript.
  • Assistant: offline templates and deterministic rules, not a generative AI model.
  • Database: browser-local only; no cloud sync or multi-user accounts.
  • Payments: not connected; checkout is simulated.
  • Shipping, tax, returns, supplier inventory: not connected.
  • Security: do not enter passwords, payment details, or sensitive customer information.
