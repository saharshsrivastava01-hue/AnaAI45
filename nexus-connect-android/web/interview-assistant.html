2. Interview preparation notes

Use truthful examples. If you have not performed a task professionally, say so and explain how you would learn or approach it.

  • Answer behavioral questions with Situation, Task, Action, Result (STAR).
  • For technical questions, explain the concept, give a simple example, and mention limitations.
  • Never claim client work, certifications, tools, or results you cannot verify.
  • Ask the interviewer about responsibilities, support, working hours, and the next step.
