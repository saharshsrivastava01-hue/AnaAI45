# Client work workflow

## Lead qualification
Capture business name, public website, contact source, stated need, contact permission / lawful basis, timezone, and do-not-contact status. Do not scrape private contact details or contact people who opted out.

## Discovery questions
- What should the website help the business achieve?
- Is there an existing WordPress site?
- Which pages or changes are needed?
- Do they have hosting, domain, branding, and content?
- What is the desired deadline?
- Who approves scope and payment?

## Proposal generation
Use the AI assistant to draft a proposal from verified facts. The operator must review price, scope, timeline, assumptions, revisions, and payment terms before sending.

## Delivery checklist
1. Confirm written scope and payment milestone.
2. Collect authorized site access through a secure password manager or temporary account; never ask clients to send passwords in chat.
3. Back up the site before changes.
4. Work in staging, not directly on production.
5. Implement only agreed changes.
6. Test mobile layout, forms, links, accessibility basics, and page speed.
7. Share preview and request client acceptance.
8. Deploy only after explicit approval.
9. Provide handover notes and remove temporary access.

## Money and payments
The app may prepare an invoice or payment request, but it must not claim funds were received until the payment provider confirms settlement. Payment processing is not included in this starter.

## AI approval policy
AI may research, summarize, draft, and create checklists. Human approval is mandatory for outbound calls, external messages, prices, contracts, purchases, access to client systems, production deployment, and refunds.
